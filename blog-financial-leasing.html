Financial Leasing for Project Financing: A Comprehensive Guide to its Advantages, Challenges, and Best Use Cases

Introduction

Turning to bank loans is often the first option that comes to mind when financing projects, but it’s not always the easiest or most suitable.

This is where "Financial Leasing" enters as a smart and flexible financing tool that allows companies to obtain the assets they need without bearing the heavy traditional debt burden.

What is Financial Leasing?

It is a contractual agreement between the "lessor" (the financing company) and the "lessee" (your project), where the lessor buys the asset and leases it to you for a specific period in exchange for periodic installments. At the end of the period, you often have the right to own the asset for a symbolic value.

Why Choose Financial Leasing? (The Advantages)

  • 1. Sustaining Cash Liquidity: Instead of paying the full price of a machine or building upfront, you keep your cash for daily operations.
  • 2. Ease of Financing: Often, leasing requirements are less stringent than bank loans, especially for startups.
  • 3. Tax Benefits: In many systems, lease installments are treated as operational expenses, which can reduce taxable income.

Challenges and Potential Risks

Despite the benefits, the total cost of leasing may be higher than the purchase price due to interest. Also, you do not own the asset until the final payment is made.

When is Financial Leasing the Ideal Choice?

Leasing is ideal when your technology needs constant updating (like computers) or when you want to expand without drying up your working capital.

Conclusion

Financial leasing offers a powerful and flexible alternative that deserves to be on every entrepreneur’s radar. It is not just about borrowing; it is a strategic management of resources and assets.
