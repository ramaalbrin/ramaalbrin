How to Create a Professional Feasibility Study in 11 Key Steps

The Practical Roadmap to Business Validation

Creating a feasibility study is the fundamental bridge that secures the transition from a mere idea into a thriving commercial business. Follow these 11 steps for a professional results:

1. Select the Economic Field

Defining whether the project is industrial, agricultural, commercial, or service-oriented to understand the applicable laws.

2. Preliminary Feasibility Study

A quick "Is it worth it?" check before spending time and money on detailed research.

3. Marketing Feasibility

Analyzing supply, demand, competitors, and defining your unique value proposition.

4. Technical and Engineering Feasibility

Determining site, machinery, production stages, and labor requirements.

5. Legal and Organizational Feasibility

Understanding the company legal structure, licenses, and organizational chart.

6. Safety and Environment Study

Assessing the project's impact on public health and the environment.

7. Financial Feasibility

Estimating initial investment costs and ongoing operational expenses.

8. Project Evaluation and Decision Criteria

Calculating Net Present Value (NPV), IRR, and Payback Period.

9. Financing Plan and Cash Flow

How will the project be funded? Debt, equity, or lease?

10. Sensitivity Analysis

What happens if costs increase by 10%? Testing the project's resilience.

11. Final Report and Recommendations

The final "Go" or "No-Go" decision based on all previous data.
